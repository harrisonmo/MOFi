## Simulation preparation

These are raw experimental structures, not prepared simulation models. Both contain partial occupancies. Resolve disorder into chemically consistent ordered models, expand symmetry, check hydrogen atoms/protonation, terminal ligands and charge balance, and establish water/ion conditions before assigning and validating simulation parameters. Preserve these raw files and save prepared structures separately.

## Capacity definitions

The abstract reports 2945 +/- 173 and 2322 +/- 28 mg/g for PFOA and PFOS on Zr-PTA1, and 375 +/- 9 and 414 +/- 41 mg/g for PFBA and PFBS on Zr-PTA2.

The supporting tables list different fitted maxima for two targets:
- Table S3, S39: Langmuir Qm(PFOA) = 2945 +/- 173 mg/g; Qm(PFOS) = 5093 +/- 796 mg/g.
- Table S6, S42: Langmuir Qm(PFBA) = 519 +/- 79 mg/g; Qm(PFBS) = 414 +/- 41 mg/g.

Do not label all four abstract values as fitted Langmuir maxima. Check the full article/isotherms to identify the comparison appropriate for each.

SI S5: aqueous batch isotherms, 25 C, 0.4 g/L MOF, 24 h, 150 rpm. Initial concentration ranges in mg/L: PFOA 0-4000, PFOS 0-3000, PFBA 0-500, PFBS 0-200. These are initial concentrations; simulation isotherms should be compared against equilibrium concentrations.

No PFAS capacity calculations have been run.
